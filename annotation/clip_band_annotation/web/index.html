Object check: crops and scenes

You will see objects that a detector found in videos from a camera worn by a young child. For each page, the detector says every object on it is one kind of thing, for example cup. Your job is to find the ones that are not.

How each page works

  1. Crops only. Click every crop that is not the named object. Clicked crops turn red; click again to undo. Leave correct ones alone.
  2. In the scene. The same crops come back in a new order, each next to its whole video frame with the object outlined in pink. Judge them again from scratch: click every one that is not the named object. Use Enlarge to see a frame bigger.

Your first answers are locked once you move to the scene step, so judge the crops on their own.

Mark as not the object

  • Other categories: a different kind of thing (a bowl on a cup page), or the box is mostly on something else.
  • Empty: no object in the box, only background (wall, floor, sky).
  • Unrecognisable: too blurry, dark or small to tell what it is.

Counts as the named object

  • Any example you can recognise, even if partly visible or seen from an odd angle.
  • Toys, pictures, drawings and screens showing it.
